How PawConnect Works

We make pet adoption transparent, organized, and stress-free for both adopters and shelter caretakers in four simple steps.

1
🔎

Search & Discover

Filter through verified pet listings by city, species, age, and temperament to find your compatible companion.

2
📝

Submit Application

Log in as an adopter and complete a simple adoption request detailing your living environment and experience.

3
🤝

Shelter Review

The shelter evaluates applications, conducts a friendly interview, and answers all your questions about pet care.

4
🏡

Welcome Home

Once approved, complete adoption formalities and welcome your new companion into a warm, safe home.
